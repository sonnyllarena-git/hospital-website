# Hospital Website Enhancement: Employee & Career Section

**Addendum to Hospital One-Stop-Shop Website Plan**

---

## Overview

Most hospital websites focus **100% on patients**. But hospitals also need to:
- ✅ Attract qualified healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, staff)
- ✅ Build employer brand (show company culture)
- ✅ Retain employees (celebrate their achievements)
- ✅ Communicate internal news (staff updates, policies, benefits)

**Your differentiator:** A hospital website that serves **BOTH patients AND employees** = more value, bigger project, higher fees.

---

## Why Add Employee/Career Section?

### Problem Hospitals Face

❌ **High staff turnover** (Philippines healthcare industry: 30-50% annual turnover)  
❌ **Difficulty recruiting** (doctors, nurses, specialists competing for same talent)  
❌ **Low employee engagement** (no internal communication channel)  
❌ **Poor employer brand** (no way to showcase company culture)

### Solution via Website

✅ **Job listings** (candidates find open positions faster)  
✅ **Employee testimonials** (builds trust, shows real work experience)  
✅ **Awards/recognition wall** (celebrates staff, boosts morale)  
✅ **Benefits showcase** (salary, health insurance, training, career growth)  
✅ **Department profiles** (new hires understand team structure)  
✅ **Blog/news** (internal updates, company announcements)

## ROI for Hospitals

### New Benefits from Employee/Career Sections

✅ **Reduced Recruitment Costs**
- Before: ₱50,000 per hire (agency fees, ads, recruiter time)
- After: ₱5,000 per hire (internal job board + application system)
- Savings: ₱45,000 × 20 hires/year = ₱900,000/year

✅ **Faster Hiring**
- Before: 60-90 days to fill a position
- After: 15-30 days (easy apply, internal talent pool first)
- Result: Less time with vacancies = better staffing

✅ **Improved Staff Retention**
- Recognition wall + career growth visibility = higher morale
- Before: 40% turnover
- After: 25% turnover (target)
- Savings: Training costs for 15 fewer replacements = ₱500,000/year

✅ **Better Employer Brand**
- "Why Work Here?" page + testimonials = attract better talent
- Candidates self-qualify (those who value culture apply)
- Result: Higher job quality, fewer bad hires

**Total First-Year ROI: ₱1,400,000+** (vs. ₱595k investment = 2.3x ROI)
